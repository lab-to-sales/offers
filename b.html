I'm building Lab to Sales: a simple, question-based training for scientists and technical people in their first commercial role. These 5 pillars are the backbone of the group cohort and custom 1:1 versions of the program — read them and notice which one would change your next 6–12 months the most.

This page is for you if you are taking over commercial responsibilities in the biotech industry and have little or no formal sales training.

This training is set to create for you skills which will last a lifetime, because it applies on your mindset and your emotional intelligence.

The five pillars
01

Business Environment Diagnosis

Before you sell anything, you need a broad perspective on your industry, where your company stands and its future. This pillar gives you the partner mindset that will separate you from other sales people and your competitors. This is necessary before you focus into understanding your clients and their needs.

  • Product Complexity Tiers
  • Technology Waves & Cycle Positioning
  • Market Context & Regulation
  • Competitive Landscape
  • Company Context
02

Strategic Focus

You have limited time and more accounts than you can handle. We use the 80/20 rule to direct your attention to valuable relationships, building a simple priority map, and making sure you are not spreading yourself too thin trying to please everyone.

  • Yearly Target & Revenue Mapping
  • Relationship Prioritization
  • Multiyear Positioning
  • Ideal Client Profile & Buyer Spectrum
03

From First Contact to Close: a complete workflow

You learn how to create and lead honest conversations within a clearly planned day-to-day workflow.

  • Prospecting & Lead Generation
  • Discovery & Qualification
  • Consultative Conversation
  • Objection Handling
  • Closing
  • Post-Sales & Account Management
04

Conversations That Matter

We turn your inner curiosity and scientific mindset into the core that connects you with your clients authentically.

  • Presence & Emotional Awareness
  • Active Listening & Questioning
  • Trust Building
  • Authentic Communication
  • Stakeholder Navigation
05

Systemise to Stay Human

We build your CRM operating system, so that your energy focuses on strategy and human interaction. We automate your operations, use AI and other automating tools for your specific use case.

  • CRM Architecture
  • Pipeline Management
  • Operational Rhythm
  • Follow-up & Cadence Systems

About me · Zoran

I am Zoran and I have more than 12 years of sales experience in price competitive and complex sales across biotech and digital marketing industries. Everyone I trained to become a senior sales manager said they learned a lot from me and I love teaching more than running pipelines.

In the lab, we know when something doesn't work. However, sales can be intimidating and highly invariable. Often we don't know what works and why. This was tough for me to handle. So I built this training by looking at the tough times of my sales career. If you are learning about sales now, I want to show you how to save effort and get closer to your targets with this training. This training is meant to be a lifetime skillset, that can expand when technology changes, but will remain as a core, as long as you talk to other human beings, when selling. Enjoy it!
